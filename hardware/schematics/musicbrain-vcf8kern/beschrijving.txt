Eight identical analog filter voices, each a four-pole SSI2140 core with pole-mixing multimode: one filter that covers low-pass, band-pass, high-pass and notch, eight response modes picked per voice in software. Cutoff and resonance for all eight come from the software brain over SPI - set together, in the same instant - and the resonance reaches self-oscillation, so every voice can double as a tunable sine source. Audio in, audio out, plus a comparator tap per voice for tuning. This is the analog filter core of the poly-analog voice path: a validation card (rev 0.1) that stands behind the VCF8 backbone. The SSI2140s are hand-soldered on top; the rest is JLC-assembled.
