The backbone of the system: one Teensy 4.1 drives every function card you plug into its six slots, and the board takes care of power, the SPI bus, MIDI (two ins, two outs), USB host and the display port. A built-in tuning input listens to a VCO while the system dials it in, and the CAN port links external satellites. Delegated boards - extra Teensys or FPGA cards - can live in any slot and merge their audio back into the master over the shared audio lines. Every MusicBrain build starts here.
