Browser-based patch editor for the Cortex modular system. You assemble a
rack from module panels, patch them with colour-coded cables for CV, gates,
triggers, audio and MIDI, and play the result right away: the built-in
engine simulates the patch polyphonically, from a single synth voice up to
an eight-voice DX7. The same patch then uploads to the Teensy over USB,
where the firmware reproduces it — a contract test keeps editor and
firmware in step, so the editor version follows the firmware series.
