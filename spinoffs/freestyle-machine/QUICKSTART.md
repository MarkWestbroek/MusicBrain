# MusicBrain Freestyle — quick start (phone)

Open **https://editor.musicbrain.nl/?app=freestyle** in Chrome (Android) or
Safari (iPhone). Use headphones or a speaker. On an iPhone the mute switch
on the side must be off, or you will hear nothing.

## Start

Tap **START**. The beat begins on the 1.

- The big number is the tempo. The four dots count 1-2-3-4.
- The four bars under the dots show where you are in the four-bar phrase.
- The screen flashes amber on the 1 of every phrase. That is your cue.

The first time, a short tour points at every button. Tap **?** at the top to
see it again.

## The buttons

| Button | What it does | When |
|---|---|---|
| **DROP** (hold) | Cuts the beat for a punchline. Let go and the beat comes back. | Out: instantly. Back: on the next beat. |
| **NEXT** | Goes to the next section: INTRO → VERSE → HOOK → BREAK → INTRO. Press it mid-line; it waits. | On the 1 of the next phrase. |
| **FILL** | A one-bar fill. On the phone it sits next to the parts. | Starts at the next bar. |
| **KICK · SNARE · HATS · BASS · MOTIF** | Mutes or unmutes that part. Tap again to cancel before it lands. Bass and hats alone is a classic breakdown. | Next bar. |
| **SAVE** | It is always recording from the first START. Was that take good? SAVE gives you the last two minutes as a WAV file. | Right away. |
| **MIC** | Adds the microphone on its own track. SAVE then gives two files, beat and voice, to mix later. Headphones keep the voice track clean. | Right away. |
| **STOP** | Plays an ending fill and stops after that bar. Press again to stop at once. The recording pauses while stopped. | Next bar, then stop. |

An amber border on a part means "this changes at the next bar". The amber
text line tells you what is coming ("→ HOOK on the 1", "HATS off next bar").

**Nothing happens on the tap itself.** Everything lands on the next beat, bar
or phrase, so press a little early and keep rapping.

## Tempo

Tap the big number in time, four taps or more. The beat follows without a
jump. The − and + buttons next to the number nudge it by 1 BPM.

## Style

Tap the style name at the top left to go to the next style: Trap half-time
(140) → Boom bap (88) → Bounce (100) → Acid hop (75, a 303 bass line in C
minor). This stops the beat; tap START again.

## Foot pedal

A Bluetooth page-turner pedal (two pedals, ◀ and ▶) works as a keyboard.
Pair it with the phone or laptop, then: **◀ = DROP** (press once, the beat
is gone; press again and it comes back on the next beat), **▶ = NEXT**. Any
mode of the pedal works (arrows or Page Up/Down).

## Autopilot

Tick **autopilot** and every phrase gets a small variation: the bass drops
out for a bar and comes back on the 1, every fourth phrase ends with a fill,
and now and then two bars of bass and hats only. One section stays alive for
minutes. Parts you switched off yourself stay off.

## Playing with someone

The key and the current chord are on screen for a guitarist (for example
"Fm · D♭" in F minor). Tick **percussionist** to thin out the hi-hats so a
djembe or shaker fits in the gaps.

## If there is no sound

- Tap START again: a phone only opens its audio after a real tap.
- iPhone: mute switch off, volume up.
- Reload the page if the tempo number does not move after START.
