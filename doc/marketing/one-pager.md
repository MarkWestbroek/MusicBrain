## De one-pager (tekstversie, EN)

# MusicBrain
**The open brain for your analog rig.**

Pedalboards, amp racks and modular synths have one thing in common: they
forget. Every setting, every routing, every patch — gone the moment you
touch it. MusicBrain gives them memory.

**One open platform, three machines:**
- 🧠 **Cortex** — polyphonic brain for modular synths. Save and recall full
  patches, play chords across your analog oscillators (up to 16 voices),
  ≤ 5 ms latency, 16-bit pitch CV. Your audio never gets digitized.
- 🎸 **Reflex** — silent relay-based pedal switcher. One tap changes your
  whole board. Configure over WiFi from any browser.
- 🔊 **Relay** — amp & speaker switching matrix for studios. Any pre to any
  power amp to any cab, pop-free, pure relays.

**Free browser editor + simulator.** Design and test patches on your laptop
before any hardware exists. Live scope included.

**100% open source (MIT).** Firmware, editor, schematics, protocols. Your
rig will never be orphaned.

**Status:** in active development — working prototypes, firmware 0.5.
Follow the build, try the simulator, or join the beta.

## LinkedIn-post (EN)

What if your pedalboard, your amp rack and your modular synth shared one
brain?

For years I've been building MusicBrain: an open-source platform that gives
analog music gear the one thing it never had — memory. Save a modular patch
and recall it tomorrow. Switch your whole pedalboard in one silent tap.
Route any amp to any cabinet from your desk.

The interesting engineering bit: it's one C++17 core driving all three
products, with a React editor that runs in the browser and a full host-side
simulator — you can design and test patches before the hardware even
exists. Audio stays 100% analog; the brain only speaks relays, CV and gate.

Everything is MIT-licensed: firmware, schematics, protocols, the lot.

We're at firmware 0.5 with working prototypes. If you're into synths,
pedals, or embedded audio engineering — I'd love your eyes on it.

## Forum-post (ModWiggler / lines / r/synthdiy — EN)

**MusicBrain — open-source (MIT) patch memory + polyphony for modular
(and a pedal switcher from the same core)**

Hi all — sharing a project I've been building in the open. MusicBrain is a
platform around one idea: analog rigs deserve save/recall.

The flagship is a modular-synth brain (Teensy 4.1): MIDI/CV routing, patch
storage, up to 16-voice allocation over your own VCOs, ≤ 5 ms note-to-CV,
16-bit pitch DACs on breakout modules, CAN-FD between cases. Audio is never
digitized — CV/gate only. There's a browser editor (WebSerial/WiFi) with a
live scope, and a host simulator so you can try the whole loop with zero
hardware. Also on the platform: digital modules incl. MS-20-style filters
and ports of Rings/Plaits, since it's all open.

Same core also runs a relay pedalboard switcher (working ESP32 prototype).

Everything is MIT: firmware, editor, schematics, protocols, ADRs. It's
pre-1.0 and I'm looking for feedback, testers and contributors.
