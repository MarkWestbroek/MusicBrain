The open brain for your analog rig.

Pedalboards, amp racks and modular synths have one thing in common: they forget. Every setting, every routing, every patch — gone the moment you touch it. MusicBrain gives them memory. Your audio stays 100% analog; the brain speaks only relays, CV and gate.

One platform · three machines

for modular synthsCortex

Save and recall full patches. Play your analog oscillators polyphonically — up to 16 voices, allocated live from MIDI.

fw 0.5 · on hardware

for pedalboardsReflex

Silent relay switching for your whole board in one tap. Configure over WiFi from any browser — no app.

working prototype

for studiosRelay

Any pre-amp to any power amp to any cab. Pop-free, pure relays, recalled from your desk.
